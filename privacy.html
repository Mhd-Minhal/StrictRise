Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Data Collection

StrictRise values your privacy. We collect essential functional data including your email and display name when you sign in via Google. For Guest accounts, all data remains local to your device.

2. Usage Data

We store your alarm configurations and history to provide the application's core functionality. History data is used locally to calculate discipline metrics and streaks.

3. Cloud Sync

If signed in with a Google Account, your alarm settings may be synced to Google Firebase to ensure you don't lose your data when changing devices. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

4. Permissions

StrictRise requires specific permissions to function correctly:

  • Exact Alarms: To trigger at the precise time you set.
  • Foreground Service: To play audio while the app is in the background.
  • Storage: Only if you choose to import custom ringtones.
  • Haptics: For sensory feedback during challenges.
